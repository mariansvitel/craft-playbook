# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation should make the project safer and more useful. Be respectful, critique
ideas and evidence rather than people, acknowledge uncertainty, and help others learn
without gatekeeping.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, exposing another person's private information, or encouraging someone to
publish secrets or unsafe material.

## Project-specific expectations

- Do not paste credentials, personal data, confidential logs, or exploit details into
  public discussions.
- Distinguish evidence from opinion and correct claims when stronger evidence appears.
- Disclose meaningful AI assistance and remain accountable for submitted work.
- Follow the security-reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect people or the project. Decisions should be
proportionate, documented where safe, and open to reconsideration when new evidence
appears.

For a moderation concern, open a minimal issue without private or sensitive details.
If private handling is necessary, ask the maintainer to establish a secure channel.
Platform-level abuse can also be reported through GitHub's own reporting tools.
